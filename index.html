About

25+ Years Experience

I've been inside the problem you're dealing with.

I'm an AI and technology consultant based in St. Johns, FL, with 25+ years building and shipping enterprise software. I've led engineering teams across healthcare, finance, insurance, and education — and I've seen the same structural problems show up everywhere.

Excel becomes the unofficial operating system. Processes get built as single-function jobs, each one punted to the next person with no visibility into what came before. Vendor apps that don't talk to each other. Jobs that ran — but did they finish? Nobody knows until something breaks.

This isn't a failure of your team. It's a structural problem — and one I've spent my career solving. As Director of Software Engineering, I defined and launched company-wide AI strategy, built agentic workflows, and delivered:

  • $2.2M+ in annual savings through AI automation and process modernization
  • Production uptime lifted from 95% to 99.99% via full Azure cloud migration
  • Delivery velocity improved 40%; release cycles cut from 8 weeks to 3
  • Built AI-driven transcription platform saving $1M+ annually
  • Recognized by the Governor of South Dakota for technology innovation

Now I bring that same thinking to small and mid-size businesses in Northeast Florida — without the enterprise price tag.
